Pump & System Curve

System Curve & Operating Point
Estimated Pump Performance Curve
How to read this: The system curve (blue) shows the head the system demands at each flow rate — it rises with flow because friction increases with the square of flow. The pump curve (amber) shows the head the pump can deliver, which drops as flow increases. Their intersection is the operating point. For pump selection, choose a pump whose curve passes through or just above your required (flow, head) point at its best efficiency point (BEP).
