NBC 2026 Guidelines (Clause 4.4.10):
• OHT only: 33.33–50% of one day's requirement
• Combined UGT + OHT (gravity): UGT = 150%, OHT = 50% of daily demand
• Hydro-pneumatic / pressurized: UGT = 200% of daily demand
• Intermittent supply (plotted dev.): OHT = half-day demand
• Apartments/hotels/hospitals/commercial: UGT = 1.5 days, OHT = 0.5 day
• STP treated water for flushing: 1 day in UGT + 0.5 day in OHT
• Firefighting storage: as per Part F (Fire & Life Safety)

Tank Design Recommendations (Clause 4.4)

  • Tanks >5,000 L should be arranged in series for isolated cleaning
  • Overflow pipe: one size larger than inlet pipe
  • Tanks >5,000 L need vent pipes
  • Outlet pipe: 50–75 mm above tank bottom with brass strainer
  • OHT bottom: minimum 2 m above highest fixture outlet
  • UGT overflow: 600 mm above external surface level
  • Overflow & scour pipes: air gap of 50 mm, mosquito-proof brass grating
  • Materials: RCC, brick masonry, ferrocement, mild/stainless steel, GRP, or plastic
