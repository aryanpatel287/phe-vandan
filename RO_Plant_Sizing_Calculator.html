.Dialysis Water — AAMI / ISO 23500-3

Critical-grade water. Dialysis water must meet AAMI/ISO 23500-3 standards. Bacterial contamination can cause pyrogenic reactions and patient mortality. This requires a multi-barrier treatment train with continuous disinfection capability.

Sterile Services Department (CSSD)

CSSD requires low-endotoxin, low-bacterial water for sterilizer feeds, washer-disinfectors, and final rinsing of surgical instruments. Endotoxin levels must remain below 0.25 EU/mL.

Laboratory & Pathology

Clinical labs require Type II (CLRW — Clinical Laboratory Reagent Water, per CLSI guidelines) for reagent preparation, analyser feeds, and glassware rinsing. Typical resistivity ≥ 1 MΩ·cm.

Kitchen & General Drinking Water

Hospital kitchen and general drinking water must meet IS 10500 + WHO. Separate distribution from critical-grade loops. Centralised drinking water stations with UV polish.
