Compliance & Method Notes

▼
  1. Storm runoff: Rational Method per IS 16220:2014 Cl 6.2 & CPHEEO Manual (Q = C·i·A/360, valid for catchments < 50 ha).
  2. Rainfall intensity 'i': Must be taken from the IMD IDF curve for the project city at the design return period and time of concentration.
  3. Channel sizing: Manning's equation V=(1/n)R^(2/3)S^(1/2); velocity kept 0.6–2.4 m/s for self-cleansing & no-scour (IS 16220 Cl 7.2.3).
  4. Fire LPM: NBC 2016 Part 4 Table 7: ≤15 m→1620; 15–45 m→2280; >45 m→2850 LPM (hydrant + sprinkler combined).
  5. Governing case: Fire water is usually the governing sump inflow in high-rises — sump pump capacity = MAX(storm, fire) × safety factor.
  6. Sump construction: Water-tight, non-clog pump, auto start/stop, NRV on discharge, silt trap before municipal sewer (IS 12251 Cl 2.2).
  7. Standby: 1 working + 1 standby (100%) pump with automatic electric changeover (IS 12251 Cl 2.4).
  8. Fire tank: Dedicated to fire (separate from domestic), sized per NBC Table 7.
