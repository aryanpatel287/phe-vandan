Applicable Standards & Codes

Indian Standards and WHO guidelines governing water softener design for public utilities.
IS 8406 : 1977
Code of practice for installation of water softeners
Vessel sizing, flow velocities (15–25 m/h), bed depth, regeneration and piping arrangement.
IS 7400 : 1994
Ion-exchange resins — specifications
Strong-acid cation (Na-form) resin capacity, physical properties and test methods.
IS 10500 : 2012
Drinking water specification
Total hardness: desirable ≤200, permissible ≤600 mg/L as CaCO₃ in absence of alternate source.
IS 1172 : 1993
Water requirements for buildings
Per-capita/per-unit demand norms used for daily flow estimation (reaffirmed 2021).
IS 2065 : 1983
Water supply, drainage & sanitation in buildings
Code of practice for building water-supply distribution and treatment.
WHO GDWQ (4th ed. + 1st add.)
Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality
No health-based limit for hardness; aesthetic/operational acceptability governs softening.
IS 3025 (Parts)
Methods of sampling & test for water
Hardness (Part 21), calcium, pH and TDS determination for feed and product water.
NBC 2016 / CPHEEO Manual
Building & water-supply norms
Demand estimation, peak factors and redundancy for public water-supply systems.
